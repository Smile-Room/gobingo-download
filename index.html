How to install

Ready in two minutes

Pick your device. After installing, open the app, tap Create My Account and log in with the email your admin gave you.

  1. Download the installerClick "Download for Windows" above and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the fileDouble-click GoBingo-Offline-Setup.exe in your Downloads folder.
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC"Click More info, then Run anyway. This appears for apps from small developers.
  4. Start GoBingoIt installs by itself and opens. Use the GoBingo shortcut on your desktop next time.
  1. Download the APKTap "Download for Android" above. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the fileTap the downloaded GoBingo-Offline.apk in the notification or in Files → Downloads.
  3. Allow the installIf asked, tap Settings → turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

Security

Your balance is protected

GoBingo is built so that only real vouchers from your admin can add balance — on your device and on our server.

✓

Encrypted data. Your games and balance are stored encrypted with a key unique to your device.

✓

Signed vouchers. Every voucher is signed by the server and works only once, only on your device.

✓

One device per account. Your account can't be taken over from another PC or phone.

✓

Tamper-proof app. The Windows app refuses to start if anyone changes its files.

Check your download (SHA-256): see SHA256SUMS.txt in the latest release.
FAQ

Questions

Do I need internet to play?

Only once, to create your account and activate offline mode. After that every game works without internet. Connect now and then so your balance limits and deposits stay in sync.

How do I add balance?

Open Payment → Offline Deposit, send your Request Code to your admin, and type the 15-character voucher you get back. With internet you can also use "Update Balance".

Can I use my account on two devices?

No. Each account works on one device. To move to a new PC or phone, ask your admin to press "Reset device" first.

Windows says the app is not recognized. Is it safe?

Yes, if you downloaded it from this page. Click "More info" → "Run anyway". You can also compare the file with the SHA-256 checksum in the release.

How do I update?

Download the newest version from this page and install it over the old one. Your account, games and balance stay.
